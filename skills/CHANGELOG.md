## 1.0.0

first release. seventeen skills.

**motion**

- `motion-system`, the decision layer: easing, duration, what to animate, and
  when not to
- `animation-craft`, pace as a claim, sequencing, blur, knowing when to stop
- `animation-performance`, the layout and paint and composite pipeline, layer
  cost, the custom property invalidation trap, frame loop details
- `css-animations`, transitions against keyframes, fill mode, hover on touch,
  seamless loops
- `animation-orchestration`, springs and velocity across retargets, measuring
  before moving, phased sequences, path morphing
- `clip-path`, reveals, comparison sliders, two color indicators, fills
- `svg-fundamentals`, viewBox, paths, stroke drawing, transform origins

**interface craft**

- `interface-polish`, type and font loading, shadows, gradients, scrollbars,
  focus, z-index, safe areas, dark mode
- `forms-and-controls`, inputs and autofill, the ios zoom rule, buttons,
  destructive actions, validation
- `touch-and-accessibility`, targets, pointer events, keyboard paths, screen
  readers, reduced motion
- `component-api-design`, composition against configuration, naming, asChild,
  refs, controlled and uncontrolled, anti-patterns

**design system**

- `design-tokens`, where tokens live, retheming, and what a distributed
  component is allowed to use

**landing pages**

- `template-architecture`, the file spine, one config, sections with no props
- `landing-page-layout`, the canvas, grid splits, padding, hierarchy
- `landing-page-copy`, headings as hooks, stats with denominators, house style
- `landing-page-performance`, budget, lazy mounting, images, client boundaries
- `landing-page-seo`, metadata factory, social cards, sitemap, structured data
