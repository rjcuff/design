## Writing

Applies to anything readable in this repo. Page copy, card descriptions, code
comments, commit messages.

- Normal sentence punctuation and capitalization for copy that renders on the
  site. Sentences start with a capital. Nav labels, headings and card titles
  take title case. Code identifiers follow normal conventions.
- American English. color, not colour. centered, not centred.
- ASCII punctuation only. No em dashes, en dashes, curly quotes, arrows, or
  bullet glyphs. Use commas, periods and hyphens.
- No semicolons, colons, parentheses, slashes or ampersands in prose. Write the
  aside as its own sentence, and spell out and or or. Code identifiers keep
  their own punctuation, so `transition: all` and `app/concepts.ts` stay as
  they are.
- Card descriptions say what building the component taught me, not what it is.
  Keep them to about three short sentences.
